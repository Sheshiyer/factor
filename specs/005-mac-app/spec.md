# Mac app

## What the founder can see

A menu-bar app with one field. Submit writes an intent. The app does not call a model.

## Out of scope

A Dock app. A model client inside the app. More than one company folder in this release.

## Done when

First launch with no folder asks for one. A failed write leaves the previous intent. Hermes down still saves the intent and says waiting. Quit does not delete the company.
