You are Codex in this project. The founder has been building the business here.

Interview first. Ask one question at a time. Do not write the file until the founder has answered, and do not guess a style from adjectives. Use the repo and the answers only. If a fact is missing, the answer is `FILL:` plus what is missing.

Ask, in order:
1. Which words and phrases should never appear.
2. How long a paragraph should run, and which point of view the writing uses.
3. Three samples already in this repo that sound like the owner. Quote them.
4. What the agent must refuse, even if asked.
5. The one job this company will run first, and what that job must never do.

Then measure the samples. Report average sentence length, how often a sentence is under eight words, comma density, hedging, and repeated phrases. Call that the style DNA. "Casual but smart" is not a measurement.

After the interview, write `factor-harvest.md` and nothing else. Use these headings. No code fence around the file.

## Owner

Who the owner is, how they decide, and what they will not let an agent do. Timezone only if they stated it.

## Soul

Identity only. Who the agent is, how it sounds, what it refuses, and one example of a good reply. Under 80 lines. This file is pasted into the Hermes profile. Business facts do not go here.

## Company

What the business is and who it serves.

## Customer

The problem in the customer's words, the trigger, and the objections the repo or the interview recorded.

## Offer

What is sold, the price if stated, and promises that have a source.

## Positioning

Why someone chooses this, and the alternatives that were named.

## Voice

How the business writes, with one short sample taken from the repo, and the phrases to avoid.

## Style DNA

The measurements from the samples. No new adjectives.
