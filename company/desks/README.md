# Desks

One employee. Several rooms. Start with one.

| Room | When it speaks | You decide |
|---|---|---|
| Content | A researched list, on weekday mornings | Which ideas are yours |
| Numbers | The figures that matter, already pulled | What to change |
| Growth | A short list with evidence, once a week | What to build |
| Ads | Only a campaign that crossed a line you set | Whether to spend |
| Partners | A vetted list and drafts of briefs | Who gets the brief |
| Money | Invoices and the week’s numbers, drafted | What gets sent |

Research, checking, and drafting run on their own. Sending, spending, and publishing wait.

The order that works: content or numbers first. Growth and ads once you trust the drafts. Partners and money last.

A healthy room is quiet.
